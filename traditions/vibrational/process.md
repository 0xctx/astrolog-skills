# Vibrational astrology — reading process

## Natal reading

1. **Check the time.** Note the Rodden rating. For anything below A, avoid harmonic angles and stay with planet
   patterns (planets are robust; angles multiply time errors).
2. **Harmonic profile.** From `strongest_harmonics` (score 1 = 4-planet patterns, then score 2 = weighted tightness),
   pick the 3–5 harmonics that stand out. Say explicitly if there are no 4-planet patterns in H1–32 (common) and that
   the reading then rests on 3-planet patterns.
3. **Each strong harmonic.** Give its quality (from `meanings.md`, with its [source]/[derived] label), then its
   patterns: which planets, how tight (natal orb), which pair is closest. Interpret the quality *through* those planets.
4. **Overtones and support.** Mention tight overtone aspects inside the harmonic chart (`astro aspects -H N`) when they
   are tighter than the pattern itself. Midpoint contacts are listed (the aspects & midpoints view) but not scored:
   mention a very tight midpoint picture only as supporting detail, never as a pattern of its own.
5. **Synthesis.** How the strong harmonics combine into a portrait: what the person is driven by (H8, H16), what they
   create (H5, H10, H25), where they turn inward (H7, H14, H21), what they need from others (H9, H17), and so on.

## Forecasting (two steps)

1. Identify the natal harmonic patterns that fit the question (e.g. elections → H8, H9, H13; marriage and healing → H9;
   introversion or isolation → H7; instability → H11; play and creativity → H5).
2. Look for transits or progressions that resonate with those patterns — planets that, in the same harmonic chart,
   join or aspect the natal pattern — and time them. (Harmonic transit timelines arrive with the forecast feature.)

## Writing rules

- Ground every claim in the data: harmonic, planets, orbs (harmonic and natal), score.
- Label derived meanings as such. Never present REVIEW.md assumptions as settled.
- Tighter patterns first; be clear when a pattern is faint (near the 16° edge).
- Describe tendencies, not destiny; no medical, legal or financial predictions.
- If blending with another tradition, keep the vibrational findings recognisable (name the harmonic) and let the
  other pack's process shape tone and structure.
