# Vibrational astrology — meanings

Original summaries of vibrational astrology (see `sources.toml`). Each harmonic is labelled:

- **[source]** — the quality named in the cited material (see `sources.toml`).
- **[theory]** — standard harmonic theory for the lowest harmonics.
- **[derived]** — built from the harmonic's prime factors, the composite principle of harmonic astrology (a harmonic
  combines the qualities of its factors; e.g. H10 = H2 × H5). Check these against your own material — see REVIEW.md.

## Principles

- **Minor aspects matter.** Every harmonic is an aspect family: H5 quintiles, H7 septiles, H9 noviles, and so on.
- **A harmonic chart is the natal chart heard at another frequency.** Planets conjunct (within 16°) in the H chart are
  in that harmonic's aspect; a harmonic also contains its multiples (H7 carries 14, 21, 28 as overtones).
- **Patterns describe behaviour.** Three planets in a pattern is a theme; four or more is behaviour you can see.
- **Tighter is stronger.** A pattern within 1–2° in the harmonic chart is very strong; near 16° it is faint.
- **Planets closest together in a pattern are the most intensely connected** and colour it most.
- **Higher harmonics are not weaker**, but more internal and less visible from outside (per the sources, at least to H180).
- **Midpoint structures** in symmetrical patterns strengthen them (the toolkit lists midpoint contacts but doesn't
  score them yet).

## Planets in patterns (short)

Sun — identity and vitality · Moon — feelings, needs, receptivity · Mercury — mind and communication ·
Venus — love, beauty, relating · Mars — action, desire, assertion · Jupiter — growth, faith, opportunity ·
Saturn — structure, discipline, limits · Uranus — independence, surprise, change · Neptune — imagination,
idealism, dissolving · Pluto — intensity, power, transformation.

A pattern's meaning is the harmonic's quality expressed *through* its planets, most strongly through the tightest pair.

## Overtones

Oppositions, squares, trines and sextiles between planets inside a harmonic chart are aspects of higher harmonics
(e.g. a trine in the H5 chart is H15; an opposition in the H7 chart is H14) — they refine, and sometimes dominate,
the conjunction patterns when their orbs are tighter.

## Timing

Harmonic positions multiply birth-time error: the Ascendant moves about 1° every 4 minutes, so in H16 a 4-minute
error shifts it 16°. Read harmonic angles only for accurately timed (AA/A) charts; planets are robust much higher.
