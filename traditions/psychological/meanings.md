# Psychological astrology — meanings

Original summaries in the modern psychological tradition (see `sources.toml` for further reading). Planets are
drives, signs are styles, houses are arenas of life, aspects are how drives talk to each other. Everything here
describes potential and tendency, never fate.

## Planets and points

- **Sun ☉** — the core self and what it is becoming; vitality, purpose, the need to be seen for who one is.
- **Moon ☽** — emotional needs, instinctive responses, what feels safe and nourishing; early bonds and home.
- **Mercury ☿** — how one thinks, learns, listens and speaks; curiosity and the style of the mind.
- **Venus ♀** — what one values and finds beautiful; how one loves, relates, harmonises and receives pleasure.
- **Mars ♂** — desire and will: how one goes after things, asserts, competes, gets angry and takes risks.
- **Jupiter ♃** — growth, faith and meaning; where life feels generous and where one tends to expand or overdo.
- **Saturn ♄** — structure, responsibility and time; where one feels tested or inadequate and slowly builds mastery.
- **Uranus ♅** — the urge for freedom, originality and sudden change; where one breaks patterns or feels different.
- **Neptune ♆** — longing, imagination and the dissolving of boundaries; inspiration, compassion, and illusion.
- **Pluto ♇** — depth, power and transformation; what is buried, intense or all-or-nothing, and how one regenerates.
- **Chiron ⚷** — a sensitive, long-standing wound and the wisdom and care that grow from living with it.
- **North Node ☊** — the direction of growth that feels unfamiliar but fulfilling.
- **South Node ☋** — familiar talents and habits one can lean on — or hide in.
- **Lilith ⚸** — the untamed, refused or exiled part of the self and its claim to autonomy.
- **Part of Fortune ⊗** — where ease, well-being and a sense of flow come most naturally.
- **Vertex Vx** — fated-feeling encounters; meetings that seem to arrive from outside.
- **Ceres ⚳, Pallas ⚴, Juno ⚵, Vesta ⚶** — nurturing and loss; pattern-seeing intelligence; commitment and equality
  in partnership; devotion and focus.
- **Ascendant** — the approach to life and first impression; how one meets the world and begins things.
- **Midheaven** — vocation, reputation and aims; what one wants to be recognised for.

## Signs

- **Aries** — direct, pioneering, impatient; energy that starts. *Fire · cardinal.*
- **Taurus** — steady, sensual, loyal, resistant to change; energy that consolidates. *Earth · fixed.*
- **Gemini** — curious, verbal, versatile, restless; energy that connects. *Air · mutable.*
- **Cancer** — protective, feeling, tenacious, home-making; energy that nurtures. *Water · cardinal.*
- **Leo** — expressive, warm, proud, generous; energy that shines. *Fire · fixed.*
- **Virgo** — discerning, useful, precise, self-critical; energy that refines. *Earth · mutable.*
- **Libra** — relational, fair, aesthetic, conflict-averse; energy that balances. *Air · cardinal.*
- **Scorpio** — intense, private, probing, loyal; energy that transforms. *Water · fixed.*
- **Sagittarius** — questing, candid, optimistic, freedom-loving; energy that seeks meaning. *Fire · mutable.*
- **Capricorn** — ambitious, disciplined, reserved, responsible; energy that builds. *Earth · cardinal.*
- **Aquarius** — independent, idealistic, detached, inventive; energy that reforms. *Air · fixed.*
- **Pisces** — empathic, imaginative, porous, devoted; energy that merges. *Water · mutable.*

**Elements**: fire — spirit, enthusiasm, action; earth — practicality, body, form; air — ideas, relating,
perspective; water — feeling, memory, intimacy. A missing or dominant element colours the whole chart.
**Modes**: cardinal — initiating; fixed — sustaining; mutable — adapting.

## Houses

1. Self, body, temperament, how one starts.  2. Resources, possessions, self-worth, talents.
3. Mind, learning, siblings, neighbourhood, everyday communication.  4. Home, roots, family, inner foundation.
5. Creativity, play, romance, children, self-expression.  6. Work routines, health habits, service, craft.
7. Partnership, marriage, open others, contracts.  8. Intimacy, shared resources, crisis, death and rebirth.
9. Beliefs, higher learning, travel, the search for meaning.  10. Vocation, reputation, authority, public life.
11. Friends, groups, hopes, the future one works toward.  12. The hidden, retreat, the unconscious, spiritual life.

## Aspects (as used by this pack)

- **Conjunction ☌** — fusion: two drives act as one, for better or worse.
- **Opposition ☍** — polarity: awareness through tension, often projected onto others until integrated.
- **Square □** — friction: inner conflict that demands action and builds strength.
- **Trine △** — flow: natural ease and talent; can be taken for granted.
- **Sextile ✶** — opportunity: cooperation that rewards effort.
- **Quincunx ⚻** — adjustment: two drives that don't understand each other and need continual fine-tuning.
- **Semisextile ⚺** — subtle awkwardness or a small, growing link.
- **Semisquare ∠ / Sesquiquadrate ⚼** — irritation and restlessness; minor squares that nag until addressed.
- **Quintile Q / Biquintile bQ** — creative talent and style; how one plays and makes things one's own.

**Tightness**: the closer the orb, the stronger and more constant the theme. Applying aspects are often felt as
developing; separating aspects as already lived.

## Patterns

- **Stellium / planet group** (3+ planets close together) — a concentrated focus of life energy in that sign or house.
- **Grand trine** — a self-contained talent loop; gifts that need a challenge to be used.
- **T-square** — a driving tension resolved through the empty leg's sign and house.
- **Grand cross** — sustained pressure producing resilience.
- **Yod** (two quincunxes to one planet) — a sense of special purpose that asks for continual adjustment.

## Retrograde

A retrograde planet's drive tends to turn inward — reflective, revisiting, individual — before it is expressed.
