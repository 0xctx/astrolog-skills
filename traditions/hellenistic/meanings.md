# Hellenistic — meanings

Summaries of the tradition's common doctrine, written for this toolkit, to interpret with. They carry no page
citations: build a cited pack from your own books or courses to read further (`astro sources add hellenistic <file>`).

## Planets

- **Sun** — rule, authority and leadership; the mind and perception; public life and action; honour and the height of fortune; dealings with the divine; the father. Head, heart, right eye. Diurnal; leads the day sect.
- **Moon** — bodily life and the body; the mother and nursing; conception; the household and lawful partnership; fortune, gains and spending; crowds; travel and wandering. Left eye, stomach, breasts. Nocturnal; leads the night sect.
- **Mercury** — learning, letters and speech; reasoning, argument and interpretation; numbers; trade, profit and banking; messages and service; siblings and the young; versatility and cunning. Takes on the colour of what it is joined to. Neutral; joins a sect as morning or evening star.
- **Venus** — desire and love; the mother and nurse; marriage and friendship; beauty, adornment, music and the refined arts; pleasure; markets and trade; gaining property; reconciliation. Neck, face, the front of the body. Benefic, nocturnal.
- **Mars** — violence and conflict; war, theft and loss of possessions; separation and exile; anger and lawsuits; wounds, fevers and burns; also boldness, command in war, work with fire and iron. Head, blood, genitals. Malefic, nocturnal.
- **Jupiter** — children and childbirth; love, alliances, friendship with the powerful; knowledge and justice; abundance, gifts, profits and inheritance; honours, offices and religious leadership; release and freedom. Thighs, feet, liver. Benefic, diurnal.
- **Saturn** — isolation, anxiety and austerity; delay, obstacles and restraint; long punishments, imprisonment, sorrow and accusation; secrets; being orphaned, unmarried or childless; also the land and farming, lasting reputation, managing others' affairs. Legs, knees, kidneys, hidden parts; illnesses of cold and damp. Malefic, diurnal.

## Configurations (as used by this pack)

- **Conjunction** — copresence in one sign: neutral in itself, it takes on the quality of the planets involved; an applying conjunction within three degrees is an adherence.
- **Sextile** — easy like the trine but weaker; malefics do little harm by it.
- **Square** — hard and the most active of the configurations; a superior square is domination.
- **Trine** — the most helpful configuration; malefics do little harm by it, benefics do their best.
- **Opposition** — adversarial; the malefics' strongest way to harm, the benefics' weakest way to help.

## Benefics, malefics and sect

The benefics tend to confirm, steady and improve what other planets signify — they say yes; the malefics tend to
deny, unsettle or spoil it — they say no. Both are tendencies on a scale: a well-placed malefic can give real
success and a badly placed benefic can bring reversals.

Day or night chart is first a question of quality: it moves each benefic and malefic along that scale rather than
making it stronger or weaker. A planet of the chart's sect works for the native, one contrary to it against them. By
day Jupiter is usually the most helpful planet and Mars the most difficult; by night Venus and Saturn.

Rejoicing is a secondary help: by hemisphere (diurnal planets above the horizon by day and below it by night, the
nocturnal ones the reverse) and by sign, in three versions. It weighs less than belonging to the sect.

## Solar phase

- Under the beams (within fifteen degrees of the Sun) a planet is hidden: its matters tend to be concealed, private
  or internal, and on the whole weakened, most of all within nine degrees. In its own domicile, exaltation or bounds
  it is protected — it rides in its chariot.
- Coming out from under the beams within a week of birth makes a planet stand out; sinking under them, it fades.
- Rising before the Sun (earlier in the zodiac) gives an active, masculine expression that shows early in life;
  rising after it a receptive, feminine one that shows later. A planet must be clear of the beams to count.
- Faster than its average motion a planet delivers sooner, slower with delay; retrograde planets delay, weaken or
  promise more than they deliver.

## Signs

Odd signs from Aries are masculine and even signs feminine — used for the sex of the people a placement describes and
for active against receptive qualities. Movable signs bring quick change, fixed signs permanence, double-bodied signs
repetition or two of a thing.

Each planet is at home in its domicile and raised in its exaltation; it is lowered in the sign opposite its
exaltation. Triplicity lords, bounds and decans divide the rulership further; a planet in its own bounds sits in a
favourable room of the house.

## Overcoming and degree

The planet on the right — earlier in the zodiac by sextile, square or trine — has the upper hand over the one on the
left. A benefic on top improves the other planet, a malefic on top spoils it. Configurations sharpen by degree and are
strongest applying within three degrees (thirteen for the Moon); applying points to what is coming, separating to what
has passed.

## Bonification and maltreatment

A planet is maltreated when a malefic dominates it by a superior square, opposes it by sign, strikes it with a ray,
engages it by an applying square or opposition, encloses it, receives its applying conjunction, or rules it from a bad
place: what it signifies is denied or corrupted. Bonification mirrors this with the benefics: what it signifies is
confirmed and improved.

The malefic contrary to the sect does the most damage and the benefic of the sect the most good. Reception — one
planet in the other's domicile — eases a maltreatment and strengthens a bonification; an exchange of domiciles is a
lesser support.

## Places

Angles (1, 4, 7, 10) are where things happen most, succedents (2, 5, 8, 11) build up to them, declines (3, 6, 9, 12)
fall away; each angle with its succedent and decline forms a triad. Places configured to the rising sign are good,
those in aversion to it bad. Planets in angles and succedents are busy, in declines idle.

1. The Helm: the native, body, life and character; beginnings.
2. Livelihood and possessions; the Gate of Hades.
3. Goddess: siblings, friends, relatives, short journeys, temples; the Moon's joy.
4. Roots: parents, home, land, inheritance; what is hidden; old age.
5. Good Fortune: children and increase; Venus's joy.
6. Bad Fortune: illness, injury, servants, enemies; Mars's joy.
7. Setting: marriage, the partner and others; later life; living abroad.
8. The Idle place: death and inheritance; inactivity.
9. God: travel, foreign lands, religion, divination; the Sun's joy.
10. Midheaven: action, occupation, reputation and rank.
11. Good Spirit: friends, alliances, hopes, gains; Jupiter's joy.
12. Bad Spirit: enemies, loss, confinement and suffering; Saturn's joy.

A place's ruler is read three ways: the topic it rules joined with the topic of the place it sits in; a good or bad
outcome as it sits in a good or bad place; and its condition — dignity, bonification or maltreatment. The ruler
usually decides the outcome more than planets sitting in the place.

## Lots

A lot gives a sign a further topic: the relation between two points, projected from the Ascendant, ties it to the
native. Judge it by its sign, its place, the planets configured to it and the condition of its lord.

- Fortune: the body, livelihood and what happens to one — possessions, reputation, privilege.
- Spirit: the soul and mind — intention, action, one's occupation.
- Eros: desire and chosen affection. Necessity: constraint, conflict, what one is forced into.
- Courage: boldness and strength, with treachery as its shadow. Victory: faith, hope, contests, success. Nemesis:
  hidden things, losses and sorrows.
- Father, Mother, Siblings, Children: that person in particular.
- Livelihood: means of living. Death: its circumstances. Accusation: chronic illness and danger, its sign naming the
  part of the body.
- Exaltation and Foundation: eminence and the stability of the life.

## Timing

The lords of the sect light's triplicity show the overall support of the life: the first lord the first part, the
second the second part, the third helping throughout; angular lords are good, succedent middling, declining poor.

Annual profections advance the rising sign one sign per year: that place is in focus for the year and its ruler is the
lord of the year.

Zodiacal releasing runs periods through the signs from the sign of Spirit (action, career, direction) or of Fortune
(body, health), each sign lasting its ruler's years.

## Glossary

- **Day and night charts** — A chart is diurnal when the Sun is above the exact Ascendant–Descendant axis, nocturnal when it is below. Sect is a matter of quality: it moves each benefic and malefic along a scale from most helpful to most difficult.
- **Of the sect** — A planet whose own sect matches the chart's works for the native: a benefic gives more freely, a malefic is restrained and can even be constructive.
- **Contrary to the sect** — A planet whose sect doesn't match the chart's works less in the native's favour: a benefic gives less, a malefic does more harm.
- **Rejoicing by hemisphere** — Diurnal planets are happier above the horizon by day and below it by night; nocturnal planets the reverse. A secondary help.
- **Rejoicing by sign** — Diurnal planets are happier in masculine signs, nocturnal planets in feminine ones (the pack's chosen variant). A secondary help.
- **Domicile** — At home in its own sign: it acts on its own terms.
- **Exaltation** — Raised up in the sign of its exaltation: honoured and able to act.
- **Depression** — Lowered in the sign opposite its exaltation.
- **Adversity** — In the sign opposite its own domicile, ruled by a planet of contrary nature; only the late tradition counts it as harmful.
- **Own bounds** — In its own segment of the sign: a favourable room in another's house.
- **Own decan** — In its own ten-degree face of the sign; used sparingly in this tradition.
- **Triplicity** — One of the three lords of the sign's element; the day or night lord leads by sect.
- **Under the beams** — Within fifteen degrees of the Sun a planet can't be seen: its matters tend to be hidden, private or weakened, most of all within nine degrees.
- **Emerging from the beams** — Coming out from under the beams within a week of birth: the planet stands out and acts strongly.
- **Sinking under the beams** — Going under the beams within a week of birth: the planet fades.
- **Chariot** — Under the beams but in its own domicile, exaltation or bounds: protected from the Sun's rays.
- **In the heart** — Within one degree of the Sun; a minority view holds such planets unharmed.
- **Morning star** — Rising before the Sun: an active expression that shows early in life.
- **Evening star** — Rising after the Sun: a receptive expression that shows later in life.
- **Additive** — Faster than its average motion: it delivers sooner.
- **Subtractive** — Slower than its average motion: it delivers with delay.
- **Retrograde** — Moving backwards: delays, weakens, or promises more than it gives.
- **Angular** — The angles (1, 4, 7, 10) are where things happen most visibly and energetically.
- **Succedent** — Succedent places (2, 5, 8, 11) build up to the next angle.
- **Declining** — Declining places (3, 6, 9, 12) fall away from an angle: less active.
- **Good place** — A place that sees the rising sign by sign: what is there works for the native.
- **Bad place** — A place in aversion to the rising sign (2, 6, 8, 12): what is there tends to work against the native.
- **Busy place** — Angles and succedents goad planets into action.
- **Idle place** — Declining places leave planets less energetic.
- **Joy** — Each planet's own place, where it expresses its best.
- **Overcoming** — A planet on the right (earlier in the zodiac) by sextile, square or trine has the upper hand over the one on its left. A benefic on top bonifies by trine or square; a malefic maltreats by a superior square, called domination.
- **Opposition trine** — A malefic maltreats by a sign-based opposition; a benefic bonifies by a superior trine.
- **Counteraction** — A planet depends on its domicile lord: ruled by a malefic in a bad place it is undermined, by a benefic in a good place supported.
- **Enclosure** — Two malefics or two benefics on either side, by body or by rays within seven degrees, with nothing between: hemmed in for worse or better.
- **Adherence** — The planet applies to a conjunction within three degrees (thirteen for the Moon): joined to what it approaches.
- **Striking with a ray** — A ray cast backwards in the zodiac strikes a planet within three degrees, strongest while still applying. A malefic strikes by square; a benefic by trine, square or sextile.
- **Engagement** — The planet applies to an exact aspect within three degrees (thirteen for the Moon): the most intense range of a configuration.
- **Reception** — One of the two is in the other's domicile: the host is kinder to its guest, so a maltreatment is softened and a bonification strengthened.
- **Lots** — A lot measures between two points and projects that distance from the Ascendant, tying their relationship to the native. Judge it by its sign, its place, the planets that see it and its lord.
- **Annual profections** — Each year of life moves the rising sign on by one sign. That place's topics come into focus and its ruler is lord of the year: what it promises natally is what the year delivers.
- **Zodiacal releasing** — Periods run through the signs from Spirit (action and direction) or Fortune (body and health), each lasting its ruler's years; the months are a twelfth of that.
- **Peak period** — A period in a sign angular to Fortune: heightened activity and visibility. The 1st and 10th from Fortune are strongest, then the 7th, then the 4th.
- **Loosing of the bond** — When the subperiods would return to the starting sign they jump to the opposite sign instead: usually a major turn in direction.
- **Completion** — After a loosing, the subperiods come back to the starting sign: things come full circle.
- **Angular triads from Fortune** — Each Fortune angle has a sign that builds up to it, the angle itself, and a sign that carries it through.
- **Triplicity lords of the sect light** — The three lords of the sect light's element show the support of the life: the first for its first part, the second for the second, the third throughout.
- **Proper face** — A planet that stands to the Sun or the Moon as its own domicile stands to theirs (Venus two signs after the Sun, as Libra is two signs after Leo): a favourable condition.
- **Running in the void** — The Moon completes no exact aspect with any planet within the next thirty degrees of its motion: an unfavourable sign of difficulty in making progress.
- **Assembly** — An applying conjunction more than three but less than fifteen degrees from exact: the two are coming together, less intensely than in an adherence.
- **Containment** — By sign: one planet's rays fall on both signs either side of a planet's sign and nothing else sees that planet — hemmed in for worse by a malefic, presumably for better by a benefic.
- **Midheaven degree** — The degree of the meridian brings the topics of action and reputation into whichever place it falls in (and the IC degree the topics of home and parents), adding to those places' own topics.
- **Mercury's role** — Mercury is neutral and takes on the role of what it is closely joined to: a benefic or malefic in its sign, configured closely by degree, or ruling its sign.
- **Busy place** — A planet in a declining place is still busy when it is trine the exact degree of the Ascendant or Midheaven within three degrees.
